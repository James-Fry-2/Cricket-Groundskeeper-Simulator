# Cricket Groundsman: playtest briefing

Thank you for helping. This page says what we're testing, what to expect, and what to send back.

## What it is
A management game about being head groundsman at a county cricket ground. Over one season you choose which strip each match is played on, prepare it with water, rollers, mowers and covers, and live with the verdicts: the match referee's rating, the captain's requests and the board's wishes.

## What we're testing
One question: **is it fun to rotate strips across a season while balancing those pressures?** We're not testing how it looks: it's text in a terminal on purpose, because the rules still change daily, and pictures come later if the idea works. Please judge the decisions, not the presentation.

There are no right answers, and you can't do it wrong. If something confuses you, that's useful to us, so please write it down.

## What it takes
- **Time:** a season takes about two to three hours. Play it in one sitting or a few: it saves itself after every turn, and asks to carry on next time you start it.
- **A Mac or a Windows PC.** The zip has everything; you don't need to install anything else.
- **Getting started:** follow README.txt in the zip to start it, then read Guide.md, which is a few pages. The game also shows tips the first time things happen.

## While you play
- **Play however you like.** Some players plan everything; some take each day as it comes. Both are fine.
- **Notes help a lot.** If anything surprises, confuses or annoys you, or if you make a choice you're torn about, jot it down in `notes.txt` in your season's folder, however rough.
- **If the game breaks or gets stuck,** note what you did just before, and send the folder anyway.

## What the game records
Each season folder holds:
- **`save.json`:** the season, so it can be replayed exactly. That's how we'll see how it played for you.
- **`telemetry.jsonl`:** a log of the commands you gave, the readings you took, how you answered requests, the screens you looked at, and how long each turn took.

Nothing about you or your computer is recorded, and nothing is sent anywhere by the game.

## What to send back
When you've finished the season (or decided to stop):
1. Zip your season's folder. It's in `Documents/Cricket Groundsman`, named `season-<date>-<number>`.
2. Answer the questionnaire. It's short, and there are no wrong answers.
3. Send both to the person who sent you the game.

If you've played more than one season, send them all and tell us which you'd like us to look at first.

Thanks again: an honest "this bit dragged" is worth more to us than politeness.
