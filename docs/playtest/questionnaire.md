# Cricket Groundsman: playtest questionnaire

About 15 minutes. Short answers are fine, and there are no wrong ones. Please answer from memory before looking anything up in the game.

## About you
1. How often do you play management or strategy games (Football Manager, city builders, and so on)? Never / sometimes / often
2. How well do you know cricket? Hardly / follow it / play or work in it
3. Mac or Windows? About how long did you play, and in how many sittings?

## The season
4. Did you finish the season and reach the review? If not, where did you stop, and why?
5. **Planning ahead.** Think about the matches in June and July. When did you decide which strip each would be played on? Did you have strips in mind for matches two or more fixtures away? How did you choose between strips?
6. **Readings.** Describe a time a reading (feel, probe or soil core) changed what you did. If none did, why not?
7. **Requests.** Describe a request from the captain or the board that you turned down on purpose. Why did you say no? If you said yes to everything, why?
8. **Verdicts.** Pick one match. How did the pitch play, and why do you think it played that way?
9. Was there a verdict, a rating or a stakeholder's reaction that felt unfair or made no sense? What happened?

## Pace and feel
10. Where did it drag, with too many turns of just pressing on? Where did too much happen at once?
11. What was the most interesting decision you made all season?
12. What was the most confusing moment?
13. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## Overall
14. Would you play another season? Yes / maybe / no, and why?
15. Anything else?
